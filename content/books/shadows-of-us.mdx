## About the Book

The past never truly leaves us. It lingers in quiet rooms, in old messages, in the exact kind of evening light that once made a promise feel permanent.

This is a story of two souls entwined by love yet separated by life's unpredictable tides. As their paths diverge, their connection remains suspended somewhere between memory and possibility. Despite the years, the echoes of their love refuse to fade, drawing them back to the shadows of what once was and what might still be.

<PullQuote>
  A deeply moving tale for anyone who has ever loved, lost, and found themselves haunted by the echoes of the past.
</PullQuote>
