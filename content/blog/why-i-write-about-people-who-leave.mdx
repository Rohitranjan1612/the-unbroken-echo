People leave in different ways.

Some leave dramatically, with doors and declarations. Some leave gently, almost politely, until one day their absence has become part of the furniture.

## The Shape of Absence

I write about people who leave because absence changes the architecture of a life. It rearranges mornings. It edits music. It teaches memory how to become both shelter and wound.

The stories are not only about loss. They are about what remains possible after loss has done its work.
