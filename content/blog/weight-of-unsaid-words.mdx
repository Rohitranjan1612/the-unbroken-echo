Some sentences never leave the throat.

They arrive fully formed, urgent and bright, and still we keep them. We tell ourselves there will be another chance. A better hour. A version of us with more courage and less fear.

## What Silence Keeps

Silence is not always empty. Sometimes it is full of everything we did not know how to carry into the open.

There are apologies that become weather. Confessions that become habits. Last messages that stay typed, unsent, glowing quietly in the dark.

<PullQuote>
  The heart keeps drafts of conversations the world never gets to read.
</PullQuote>

Maybe writing is one way of returning to those rooms with a little more mercy. Maybe every story is an attempt to say, at last, what could not be said in time.
