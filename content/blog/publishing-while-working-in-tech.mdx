Most writing happened in the margins.

Not the romantic kind of margins. Real ones: between meetings, after deployments, on weekends that were supposed to be empty but somehow carried laundry, errands, and exhaustion.

## Systems Help

Engineering taught me to respect systems. A book can feel mystical from the outside, but from the inside it is also a long sequence of small repeatable actions.

Draft. Rest. Revise. Cut. Return. Keep going.

<Callout>
  The trick was not waiting for perfect inspiration. The trick was making the page easy to return to.
</Callout>

Writing while working full-time did not make the book easier. It made the book more honest about what persistence actually looks like.
